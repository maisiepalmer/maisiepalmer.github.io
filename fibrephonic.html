fibrephonic

about photos process

Composition, costumes, and choreography are essential elements of musical performance — yet for many performers with physical disabilities, these elements remain inaccessible. Fibrephonic is a research project exploring how wearable technology, specifically data gloves embedded with fibre optic sensors, can create new interfaces for musical expression.

The project sits at the intersection of HCI, music technology, and disability studies, asking: what does it mean to design instruments for bodies that fall outside the norm? How do we build tools that are expressive rather than merely functional?
