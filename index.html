About

I’m a practice-based researcher exploring the intersection of costume, sound, and movement through sensor-integrated textiles and performance.

Research

My PhD investigates how wearables can transform live performance through embodied interaction. The research combines design, electronics, and sound composition, informed by feminist design theory and new materialism.

Projects

  • Interactive Costume Study (2025): A workshop series using IMUs and gesture-based sound design.
  • Musical Fabric Toolkit: Ongoing development of a sewing-based sensor system for creative practitioners.
