bio

Maisie (she/her) is a Postgraduate Researcher in the School of Computing and Creative Technologies at Leeds Beckett University, working at the intersection of music performance, interactivity, and accessibility.

Her current research project, Fibrephonic, investigates how wearable technology and data gloves can open up new forms of musical expression — particularly for performers with limited motor control.

She is also a collaborator on Junonuno, a live electronic music project with Finn Coomarasamy.
