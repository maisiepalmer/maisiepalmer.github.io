bio

Maisie (she/her) is a Postgraduate Researcher in the School of Computing and Creative Technologies. She earned her degree in Audio and Music Technology from the University of the West of England (UWE), specialising in audio programming, live performance broadcast, and methods for the visualisation of musical form and structure. During her studies, she gained valuable research experience through internships on various projects, developing skills in study design, research methods, and academic writing.

Her current research project, Fibrephonic, investigates the intersection of music performance, interactivity, and accessibility. By combining traditional textile fabrication techniques with interactive performance, Fibrephonic enables artists to design modular performance costumes through the augmentation of fabric surface texture. This holistic approach facilitates a unified process for costume design, music composition, and movement choreography, which are traditionally treated as separate artistic practices, promoting a more integrated and collaborative creative process across disciplines.
