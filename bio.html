bio

Maisie (she/her) is a Postgraduate Researcher in the School of Computing and Creative Technologies at the University of the West of England (UWE Bristol), working at the intersection of music performance, interactivity, and accessibility.

Her current project, Fibrephonic, combines traditional textile fabrication with interactive technology to create modular performance costumes that respond to movement. The work brings together costume design, music composition, and choreography into a unified creative process — rethinking how we design for musical performance, and for whom.
